How is fuel cost calculated?

Fuel required is calculated by dividing the distance by the vehicle's mileage. Multiply the fuel required by the price per litre to estimate fuel cost.

Fuel cost = Distance ÷ Mileage × Fuel price

For example, a 250 km drive at 15 km/l with fuel at ₹105 per litre needs about 16.67 litres and costs approximately ₹1,750.

Tips for a more useful estimate

  • Use real-world mileage rather than only the advertised figure.
  • Enter the current price from your usual fuel station.
  • For a return trip, enter the full distance.
