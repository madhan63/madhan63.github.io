10 Practical Ways to Improve Car Mileage in India

Fuel expenses can add up quickly, especially in stop-and-go city traffic. Although mileage depends on your vehicle and driving conditions, a few sensible habits can help you use fuel more efficiently.

1. Accelerate smoothly

Avoid repeatedly accelerating hard and braking suddenly. Look ahead, maintain a safe following distance and build speed smoothly when traffic allows.

2. Check tyre pressure regularly

Underinflated tyres can increase rolling resistance. Check pressure when the tyres are cold and follow the pressure recommended by your vehicle manufacturer, usually listed in the owner's manual or on the vehicle's tyre-pressure label.

3. Reduce unnecessary weight

Remove heavy items you do not need for the journey. Extra weight can increase fuel use, particularly during frequent acceleration.

4. Avoid unnecessary idling

When safely parked, avoid leaving the engine running for long periods without a reason. Follow your vehicle manual and local traffic rules.

5. Maintain your vehicle

Follow the recommended service schedule. Worn components, incorrect engine oil and neglected maintenance can affect efficiency and reliability.

6. Plan your route

Compare routes before leaving and consider traffic, road conditions and distance. The shortest route is not always the quickest or most fuel-efficient.

7. Use air conditioning sensibly

Air conditioning can increase fuel consumption. Use a comfortable setting and follow the owner's manual for effective operation in hot weather.

8. Avoid aggressive driving

Frequent hard braking and rapid acceleration waste energy. Drive defensively and anticipate traffic changes while maintaining safe speeds.

9. Track your actual mileage

Record distance travelled and fuel used over comparable journeys. Divide kilometres travelled by litres consumed to calculate km/l.

10. Compare fuel costs before a trip

Estimate your fuel requirement before travelling so you can budget realistically. Use your own observed mileage rather than assuming the manufacturer's claimed figure will match every journey.

How much could you save?

Your savings depend on your starting mileage, distance travelled, fuel price and driving conditions. Avoid assuming a fixed percentage improvement: track your own results over several comparable trips.

Frequently asked questions

Does tyre pressure affect mileage?

Yes. Tyres below the recommended pressure can increase rolling resistance. Do not overinflate them; use the manufacturer's recommended pressure.

Does city driving reduce mileage?

Frequent stops, congestion and repeated acceleration can reduce mileage compared with steady driving on an open road.

How do I calculate my car's mileage?

Divide the distance travelled in kilometres by the litres of fuel consumed over that distance.

Note: These are general driving tips. Results vary by vehicle, maintenance, traffic, weather and driving conditions. Always prioritise road safety.
