ABOUT US

Driving costs, made clearer.

DriveCost India is a simple independent website concept built to help drivers estimate fuel expenses, understand real-world mileage and plan road-trip budgets.

Our calculators use the numbers entered by visitors. Fuel prices and vehicle efficiency vary, so results are estimates rather than promises of actual expenditure.

Our approach

  • Keep calculators simple and easy to use.
  • Explain formulas and assumptions.
  • Avoid presenting estimates as verified vehicle specifications.
  • Improve the tools based on useful feedback.

This is an independent information project and is not affiliated with any vehicle manufacturer or fuel company.
