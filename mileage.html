Mileage can vary with traffic, tyre pressure, load, weather and driving style.

How to calculate mileage

Divide the distance travelled by the fuel consumed during that same period.

Mileage (km/l) = Distance travelled ÷ Litres consumed

For example, if a vehicle travels 300 km using 20 litres of fuel, its mileage is 15 km/l.

Improve measurement accuracy

  • Measure over a meaningful distance.
  • Use the same refuelling method each time where practical.
  • Compare similar routes and driving conditions.
