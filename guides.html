FUEL BUDGETING

How to estimate fuel cost for a journey

Start with your total distance, your vehicle's real-world mileage and the price per litre. Divide distance by mileage to estimate fuel needed, then multiply by the price.

Example: 250 km ÷ 15 km/l × ₹105/l = about ₹1,750.

VEHICLE EFFICIENCY

How to measure real-world mileage

Track the distance travelled and the fuel consumed over the same period. Divide kilometres by litres to estimate km/l. Results vary with traffic, tyre pressure, load and driving style.

ROAD TRIPS

Build a simple road-trip budget

Estimate fuel first, then add known tolls, parking and other planned expenses. Keep a buffer for unplanned stops and route changes. Always check current toll information before travelling.
